    How HomeRule works

    HomeRule reads housing laws once, turns them into dated rules, and checks each rule against this building’s facts. Every answer quotes the law and shows its date. When a fact is missing, it says so instead of guessing. When two rules may conflict, it shows both and flags it; it doesn’t decide.

    Not legal advice: HomeRule shows what published rules say, not how they apply to your own case. Demo data: hand-prepared from the challenge brief; quotes are verbatim excerpts from the source documents. Building facts come from public property records.
