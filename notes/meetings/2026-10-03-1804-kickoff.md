## c2 / RealPage: what was said about building it and how to differentiate

**Why it fits**

- Dimitar: it is "a data curation and retrieval problem", where the team has the most background. "The most powerful" challenge, and "something that we know".

**Evolutionary harness optimisation (Dimitar's main idea)**

- He pointed to the Prime Intellect agent framework and an NVIDIA framework (name heard as "AIGO") [unclear].
- How it works: a coding agent builds harness variants, these are scored on a benchmark, and evolutionary selection over train/test splits picks what moves forward. It can create tools, not only prompts.
- He has wanted to try this at work but had no benchmark. RealPage provides both a corpus and a benchmark, which makes it possible here.
- This is the root of proposal point (2), the self-improving loop scored by score.py.

**Layered jurisdiction map (Silvan)**

- Geocode the address, intersect the layers (federal, state, county, city, district), and attach each rule to the layer it is valid for through metadata. Dimitar: "it would all come down to metadata". "We have to cut hard" on depth.
- Silvan explained it with a Zurich example (Kreis 5, city, canton, Switzerland).
- Silvan later noticed the brief's "resolve" step is "exactly what I tried to explain".
- A map visual for the demo (Dimitar).

**Agentic crawler with vetting and a change log (Silvan)**

- Judges source quality, checks whether something has been overridden, deprecates old law and keeps a change log.
- Silvan expects finding and judging sources to be the hard part. Dimitar: data management is "the worst part"; with good retrieval "the rest is kind of trivial".
- Parsing with Docling (Dimitar; heard as "dockling").
- Note: this was said before they saw that the 87 texts are provided. The crawler is probably lower priority now [unclear].

**Access through MCP or a custom GPT**

- Silvan picked this up from the funnel output: "a very neat way to access it, besides just a website with a chatbot." Dimitar: "we need to come up with something that's more than that for sure."

**Housing advice clinics as the target user**

- Came from a funnel idea, and Dimitar explained it: pro bono intake, for example a volunteer preparing a short appointment for a renter facing eviction.

**Court precedent map**

- Track which ruling supersedes which (Dimitar, from a funnel idea). He called it "technically cool". It was not pursued.

**No legal advice**

- Dimitar: say what the law says and don't let the agent claim "this is how it applies".
- Silvan: show the uncertainty and say "check with your lawyer".
- Dimitar: "prepare a brief so your law consultation is more targeted ... you don't waste money."
- This is close to proposal point (3), turning "unknown" into the one question to ask.
